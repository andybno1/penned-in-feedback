# Penned In - Bug Reports & Feature Requests

This is where to report bugs and suggest ideas for **Penned In**, the Minecraft mod that keeps a whole herd of animals in one block.

Download the mod from [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/penned-in).

## Found a bug?

1. Check you're on the **latest version** of Penned In. Your bug may already be fixed.
2. Look through the [open issues](https://github.com/andybno1/penned-in-feedback/issues) in case someone has already reported it. If they have, add a comment there instead.
3. Open a **[Bug report](https://github.com/andybno1/penned-in-feedback/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml)** and fill in the form.

Logs help more than anything else. Upload your `latest.log` (in your game folder's `logs` folder) to [mclo.gs](https://mclo.gs) and paste the link into the form.

## Got an idea?

Open a **[Feature request](https://github.com/andybno1/penned-in-feedback/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml)**. Ideas for new pen designs, support for animals from other mods, and quality-of-life changes are all welcome.
